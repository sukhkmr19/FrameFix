Terms of Use

These terms describe how you may use FrameFix, a browser-based image and video inpainting tool. By using the site, you agree to them.

1. Use only content you may edit

You must own the image or video, or have permission from the rights holder to edit it. Do not use FrameFix to bypass paid platform branding, DRM, licensing restrictions, or another party’s rights.

2. How processing works

Image and video processing happens locally in your browser. FrameFix does not upload your media to a remote service. The ONNX Runtime code and WebAssembly files are bundled with this site. Your browser downloads the AI model from its configured public host; that host may receive ordinary network request information but does not receive your media from FrameFix. If you run the optional local proof utility, the finished export is transferred only to a helper listening on this same computer (127.0.0.1). It hashes the export in memory and signs a separate proof; it does not store or forward the media. The proof uses the local computer clock and does not independently attest that AI processing occurred or establish original authorship. The image or video pixels are not changed to embed the proof. The utility is provided in the public repository for users who want to inspect or run it.

3. Results and limitations

AI inpainting estimates pixels that are hidden by a mark; it cannot guarantee recovery of the original pixels. Images are processed once. Videos are processed one decoded frame at a time, which takes longer and may produce slight variation between frames. The selected region stays at a fixed screen position. Review the result before using or sharing it.

4. No account or payment

The current version does not require an account and does not collect a fee. Any future account, plan, or payment feature will require updated terms before it is offered.

5. Availability

The site and its features are provided as available. Browser support, device memory, model availability, and export codecs can affect processing. Keep your original file and check the exported image or video before relying on it.
